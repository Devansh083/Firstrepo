01 · Featured research

Multiport Bidirectional DC-DC Converter with Asymmetrical Duty-Cycle Limit Control (A-DLC) for EV Microgrids

Research Intern · Centre for Smart Grid Technologies, VIT Chennai · Mar 2026 – present

What I did

  1. Replicated the base paper. Built the IEEE base-paper converter in MATLAB/Simulink (Simscape Electrical) and checked it open loop.
  2. Closed the loop. Designed the boost-mode controller: duty-cycle determination, PI voltage/current loops and limiters. I tuned and debugged it until the results matched the paper within roughly 1–3%.
  3. Validated performance. Reached about 98.5% efficiency at 400 V output and also validated 800 V output operation, all in simulation.
  4. Adapted it for EVs. Rebuilt the design as a hybrid energy storage system (HESS): Port 1 is the battery (input), Port 2 is a supercapacitor, and the output is the battery.
  5. Moved to real time. Ran the modified model on an OPAL-RT OP4610XG real-time simulator.
  6. Documented the results. Logged all open- and closed-loop data and produced IEEE-style efficiency vs output-power plots in Python/Matplotlib.

Status A journal manuscript (MATLAB + OPAL-RT results) is in preparation.

ESP32 Solar Panel Health Monitor

An ESP32 node that reads light (BH1750), panel voltage (0–25 V sensor) and temperature (DHT22). Threshold rules detect abnormal conditions and show maintenance messages on an OLED. A small feed-forward neural network, exported to TensorFlow Lite and a C header, supports and cross-checks those rule-based decisions.
