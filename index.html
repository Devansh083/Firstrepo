Multiport Bidirectional DC-DC Converter with A-DLC Control

Asymmetrical Duty-Cycle Limit Control on an interleaved, SiC-based converter with 72 V / 48 V low-voltage ports and a 400 V / 800 V high-voltage port, after an IEEE Transactions base paper.

Mar – May 2026

Replicated the base paper in MATLAB/Simulink

Built the full converter in Simscape Electrical. Open-loop buck mode reached about 98% efficiency. In boost mode I traced and debugged the loss sources until the model behaved like the paper.

Summer 2026 · internship

Designed the closed-loop boost controller

Duty-cycle determination, PI voltage/current loops and limiters, tuned until results matched the paper within ~1–3%. Reached about 98.5% efficiency at 400 V and validated 800 V operation. All open- and closed-loop data is logged in Excel.

Results ready, paper pending

Battery + Supercapacitor Hybrid Energy Storage (EV setup)

The same converter, re-architected as a HESS for an EV environment.

2026

Adapted the converter for an EV

Port 1 (72 V) became the battery, Port 2 (48 V) the supercapacitor, and the output the EV battery. I studied performance against supercapacitor voltage in boost and buck, and made IEEE-style efficiency vs output-power plots for 400 V and 800 V in Python/Matplotlib.

2026 – present

Validated it in real time on OPAL-RT

Ran the HESS model on an OPAL-RT OP4610XG real-time simulator and obtained results. Next is a conference paper, followed by a full-system journal manuscript, which is being written now.

Embedded · Sensing · Applied ML

ESP32 Solar Panel Health Monitor

An ESP32 with a BH1750 light sensor, a 0–25 V voltage sensor, a DHT22 and an OLED. A threshold-based health model compares irradiance with expected panel voltage and applies temperature derating. A multi-page OLED UI shows maintenance messages.

I logged a labelled dataset and trained a feed-forward neural network, which I exported to TensorFlow Lite and a C header. The rules make the decisions; the network supports and validates them.
